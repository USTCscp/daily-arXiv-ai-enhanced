### [1] [APEX survey of interstellar HCl: $^{35}$Cl/$^{37}$Cl isotopic ratios in dense cores and outflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21813)

Overall, the results suggest that most Galactic chlorine was synthesized during epochs of lower average metallicity in the Galaxy. Notably, we detect H$^{35}$Cl emission arising from outflows - particularly explosive ones - hinting at its presence in a broader range of environments. This work establishes astrometric microlensing with Gaia as a powerful, complementary, and near-future probe with the potential to discover exotic astrophysical dark objects.

Method: 使用Aquarius模拟中的五个银河系质量晕，引入信息论框架，通过位置与金属性的互信息量化空间-化学相关性。将恒星分为高金属性和低金属性群体，分析其金属性分布函数、空间各向异性以及角位置-金属性耦合随半径的变化。

Result: 金属性分布函数表现出显著多样性，从单一峰值分布到宽峰或双峰分布，揭示了晕组装的随机性。低金属性恒星（主要来自瓦解的卫星星系）表现出更高的空间各向异性和更强的角聚类。移除束缚卫星后各向异性显著降低，但高金属性恒星仍保持轻微各向异性。位置与金属性的互信息随半径增加后在外围饱和，数据与随机对照的差异主要在内晕，表明不完全相混合驱动的残余空间-化学耦合。

Conclusion: 信息论诊断为量化恒星晕的化学复杂性提供了强大而直观的方法，为比较模拟与未来高维银河系巡天数据提供了有前景的途径，能够揭示星系组装历史的化学和动力学印记。

Abstract: The metallicity structure of stellar halos encodes the fossil record of galaxy assembly, tracing the chemical evolution and dynamical imprint of past mergers. Using five Milky Way-mass halos from the Aquarius simulations, we introduce an information-theoretic framework to quantify spatial-chemical correlations through the mutual information (MI) between angular position and metallicity. We divide stars in each halo into high- and low-metallicity populations based on their median metallicity and examine their metallicity distribution functions (MDFs), spatial anisotropies, and angular-metallicity couplings as a function of galactocentric radius. The MDFs exhibit remarkable diversity, ranging from single-peaked distributions dominated by one or two massive progenitors to broad or bimodal forms shaped by multiple accretion events, revealing the stochastic nature of halo assembly. The low-metallicity stars, primarily contributed by disrupted satellites, display higher spatial anisotropy and stronger angular clustering than their metal-rich counterparts. After removing bound satellites, anisotropy decreases significantly, yet high-metallicity stars remain marginally more anisotropic, reflecting the lingering debris of massive, centrally deposited progenitors. The mutual information between angular position and metallicity increases with radius before saturating in the outskirts, with the difference between the data and randomized controls confined mainly to the inner halo signifying residual spatial-chemical coupling driven by incomplete phase mixing. Our results demonstrate that information-theoretic diagnostics provide a powerful and intuitive way to quantify the chemical complexity of stellar halos and offer a promising route to compare simulations with forthcoming high-dimensional Galactic survey data.
