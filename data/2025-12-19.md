Motivation: 研究再电离时期LyC光子逃逸机制，通过分析高红移矮星系的Lyα发射线特性来识别LyC逃逸候选体，理解早期宇宙中星系对再电离的贡献。

Method: 利用JWST/NIRCam无缝光谱观测引力透镜星系团A2744中的矮星系AMORE6，分析其Lyα发射线的轮廓、速度偏移、半高宽和对称性等特性，推断中性氢柱密度和辐射传输效应。

Result: AMORE6的Lyα发射线几乎对称且尖锐（FWHM=58±1 km/s），相对于系统红移的速度偏移极小（dv=4±67 km/s），表明中性氢柱密度低，光学薄介质，与LyC光子逃逸兼容。

Conclusion: AMORE6是再电离时期最引人注目的LyC逃逸候选体之一，其Lyα特性表明可能存在非零的电离光子逃逸分数，为研究早期宇宙星系对再电离的贡献提供了重要线索。

Abstract: We report the unprecedented Ly$α$ properties of AMORE6, an extremely metal-poor ($12+\log({\rm O/H}) < 6$), low-mass ($M_\star = 4.4\times10^{5}\,M_\odot$), and ultra-compact (effective radius $\lesssim30$ pc) dwarf galaxy at $z=5.7253$, gravitationally lensed by the cluster A2744. A prominent, narrow, and nearly-symmetric Ly$α$ emission line is detected at the systemic redshift (the latter traced by H$β$, from JWST/NIRCam slitless spectroscopy), with rest-frame $EW=150\pm10$ Å, $\rm FWHM=58\pm1$ km s$^{-1}$, and a slight asymmetry, resulting in a $\rm \sim10\%$ flux excess in the red wing of the line. The negligible velocity offset from systemic ($dv = 4\pm67$ km s$^{-1}$, $3σ$ uncertainty), together with the sharpness and symmetry of the profile, indicates minimal radiative transfer effects implying a neutral hydrogen column density consistent with an optically thin medium, compatible with a non-zero ionizing photon escape fraction. If indirect spectral diagnostics calibrated at $z<4.5$ remain the only viable tools to identify LyC leakers during reionization, then based on its strongest indicator (Ly$α$), AMORE6 stands out as one of the most compelling LyC-leaking candidates yet discovered in the reionization epoch.
