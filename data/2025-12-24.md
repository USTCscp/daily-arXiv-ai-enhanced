### [1] [K-DRIFT Science Theme: Illuminating the Next Era of Galaxy Cluster Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19787)
*Jaewon Yoo,Kyungwon Chun,Jongwan Ko,Jihye Shin,Cristiano G. Sabiu,Jaehyun Lee,Kwang-il Seon,Jae-Woo Kim,Jinsu Rhee,Sungryong Hong,Woowon Byun,Hyowon Kim,Sang-Hyun Chun,Hong Soo Park,Yongmin Yoon,Jeehye Shin*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: K-DRIFT望远镜专为观测低表面亮度天体设计，可用于研究星系团内光、暗物质分布及各类LSB天体，结合机器学习与多波段巡天协同观测。

Motivation: 探索星系团内低表面亮度现象，特别是星系团内光与暗物质的空间分布关系，以及星系团环境中各类LSB天体的演化机制。

Method: 采用专门的LSB观测与分析技术，结合定制化模拟和机器学习方法，并与多波段巡天项目协同观测。

Result: 提出了K-DRIFT望远镜的科学目标和技术方案，展示了其在研究星系团内光、暗物质分布及LSB天体演化方面的独特能力。

### [2] [Obtaining Magnetization of Super-Alfvénic Turbulence with the Structure Functions of Gradient Directions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19816)
*A. Lazarian,Yue Hu,D. Pogosyan*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 提出一种基于梯度方向结构函数的新诊断方法，用于识别超阿尔芬湍流中的磁化转变尺度，并改进传统DCF方法在超阿尔芬区域的磁场强度估计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超阿尔芬湍流在星际介质和星系团等离子体等天体物理环境中普遍存在，但传统Davis-Chandrasekhar-Fermi方法在超阿尔芬区域失效，需要新的诊断工具来识别磁化转变尺度和估计磁场强度。

Method: 引入基于梯度方向结构函数的新诊断方法，可直接从光谱和同步辐射强度观测中获取，用于识别湍流从流体主导到磁流体主导的转变尺度l_A，并推导出适用于超阿尔芬区域的广义磁场强度估计表达式。

Result: 新方法能稳健地恢复转变尺度l_A，提出的广义表达式成功替代了传统DCF方法在超阿尔芬区域的应用，并展示了如何从同步辐射强度图推断扩散介质（如星系团内介质）的磁化状态和磁场强度。

Conclusion: 该研究为超阿尔芬湍流环境提供了一种有效的诊断工具，改进了磁场强度的估计方法，并通过数值模拟验证了理论预测，对理解天体物理环境中的磁化过程具有重要意义。

Abstract: Super-Alfvénic turbulence is widespread in astrophysical environments, including molecular clouds and the diffuse plasma of galaxy clusters. At large scales, magnetic fields play only a minor dynamical role; however, for sufficiently extended turbulent cascades, the motions transition into the MHD regime at a characteristic scale $l_A$. We introduce a new diagnostic based on the structure functions of the gradient directions, which can be obtained directly from spectroscopic and synchrotron intensity observations. We demonstrate that the new measure robustly recovers the transition scale $l_A$. Building on this result, we propose a generalized expression that replaces the traditional Davis-Chandrasekhar-Fermi (DCF) method for estimating magnetic field strength in the super-Alfvénic regime, where the DCF approach fails. We further show how the magnetization and magnetic field strength of diffuse media, such as the intracluster medium, can be inferred using synchrotron intensity maps. Our theoretical predictions are validated through numerical simulations.

### [3] [Microlensing Black Hole Shadows-II: Constraining Primordial Black Hole Dark Matter using the photon rings of M87 and Sgr A*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20037)
*Himanshu Verma,Priyanka Sarmah,Joseph Silk,Kingman Cheung*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 该研究扩展了微引力透镜理论框架，利用黑洞光子环的微引力透镜畸变来约束原初黑洞暗物质，分析了Sgr A*和M87*的观测潜力，发现M87*能提供最强的约束，未来EHT级干涉仪可探测到PBH暗物质分数低至1%的信号。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着EHT级干涉仪分辨率的提升，解析Sgr A*和M87*的光子环成为可能。这为利用微引力透镜引起的黑洞阴影畸变来探测原初黑洞暗物质提供了新机遇，特别是当PBH作为暗物质候选体时，其微引力透镜效应会在光子环上产生可观测的时变不对称性。

Method: 扩展先前开发的微引力透镜理论框架，应用于Sgr A*和M87*系统。考虑三种PBH种群：(1)中心超大质量黑洞周围的PBH暗物质尖峰；(2)银河系和M87星系中的NFW晕贡献；(3)前景银河系PBH暗物质对M87*观测的影响。分析微引力透镜信号如何表现为光子环的时变不对称性和形变，评估未来EHT级干涉仪的探测能力。

Result: M87*能提供对PBH暗物质最强的约束。研究表明，在0.1微角秒角分辨率下，若未观测到光子环不对称性，可约束质量范围10^{-5}M⊙到10^{6}M⊙的PBH，最大灵敏度在10^{3}M⊙附近，对PBH暗物质分数的约束可低至f_{PBH}~10^{-2}（1%）。

Conclusion: 利用黑洞光子环的微引力透镜畸变为探测原初黑洞暗物质提供了强有力的新方法。M87*是约束PBH暗物质的最佳目标，未来高分辨率干涉观测有望对PBH暗物质参数空间施加严格限制，特别是中等质量范围的PBH。

Abstract: The resolution of photon rings of Sgr~A$^*$ and M87 is the next milestone of upcoming EHT-like interferometries. We extend the formalism developed in our previous work~\cite{Verma:2023hes} to constrain primordial black hole (PBH) dark matter using microlensing-induced distortions of black hole shadows. Building upon the theoretical framework for microlensing of photon rings, we apply this methodology to both Sgr A* and M87, considering multiple PBH populations: (i) PBH dark matter spikes around central supermassive black holes, (ii) NFW halo contributions in the Milky Way and M87 galaxies, and (iii) foreground Milky Way PBH dark matter affecting M87* observations. The microlensing signal manifests as a time-dependent asymmetry and deformation of the photon ring, providing the most sensitive observable for lensing effects. We assess the detectability of these signatures with future EHT-like interferometers. Our analysis reveals that M87* provides the strongest constraints on PBH dark matter. We show that the absence of photon-ring asymmetries in observations with angular resolution of order $0.1\,μ{\rm as}$ can constrain PBHs in the mass range $10^{-5}\,M_\odot \lesssim M_{\rm PBH} \lesssim 10^{6}\,M_\odot$, with maximal sensitivity near $M_{\rm PBH}\sim10^{3}\,M_\odot$, for PBH dark matter fractions as small as $f_{\rm PBH}\sim10^{-2}$.

### [4] [How Mergers and Flybys Shape Azimuthal Age Patterns in Spiral Galaxies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20241)
*Qian-Hui Chen,Alex M. Garcia,Zefeng Li,Kathryn Grasha,Emily Wisnioski,Paul Torrey,Rhea-Silvia Remus,Lucas C. Kimmig,Andrew J. Battisti,Sven Buder*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 利用Auriga宇宙学模拟研究银河系质量旋涡星系中年轻恒星（<20亿年）的方位角年龄分布，发现旋臂前缘通常比后缘年轻30-80百万年，但气体丰富的相互作用会破坏这种年龄偏移，星系相互作用后约6亿年可恢复典型年龄模式。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 旋涡结构是星系中最常见的特征之一，但其起源和演化仍存在争议。恒星年龄分布为星系演化和恒星形成提供了重要线索，但环境效应可能掩盖内在的年龄模式。本研究旨在通过宇宙学模拟探究旋涡星系中年轻恒星的方位角年龄分布及其受环境相互作用的影响。

Method: 使用Auriga宇宙学重力-磁流体动力学放大模拟，研究五个银河系质量旋涡星系在过去50亿年内的年轻恒星（<20亿年）方位角年龄分布。通过平均年龄偏移（Δτ）和非重叠分数（f_non-overlap）量化旋臂两侧的年龄梯度，并分析合并和飞越事件对年龄梯度的影响。

Result: Auriga旋涡星系通常在其前缘比后缘拥有更年轻的恒星，典型Δτ在30-80百万年之间。然而，气体丰富的相互作用会破坏这种年龄偏移，导致旋臂两侧具有相似的年龄分布。在三个快照中观察到旋臂两侧平均年龄相似但年龄分布宽度不同，这与卫星相互作用穿过主星系盘面的事件相吻合。模拟数据表明，典型的方位角年龄变化在星系相互作用后约600百万年内恢复。

Conclusion: 这项研究强调了环境相互作用在塑造旋臂年龄模式中的短暂作用。旋涡星系通常表现出前缘更年轻的年龄梯度，但星系相互作用可以暂时破坏这种模式，随后在相对较短的时间内恢复。这为理解旋涡结构的演化和环境对星系内部结构的影响提供了重要见解。

Abstract: Spiral structures are one of the most common features in galaxies, yet their origins and evolution remain debated. Stellar age distributions offer crucial insights into galaxy evolution and star formation, though environmental effects can obscure the intrinsic age patterns. Using the Auriga cosmological gravo-magnetohydrodynamical zoom-in simulations, we investigate the azimuthal age distribution of young stars (<2 Gyr) in a sample of five Milky Way-mass spiral galaxies over the past 5 Gyr. We quantify the age gradients across spiral arms using the mean age offset ($Δτ$) and the non-overlap fraction ($f_{non-overlap}$). We further analyse the impact of mergers and fly-by events on the age gradients. Our results show that Auriga spiral galaxies generally feature younger stars in their leading edges compared to the trailing edges, with a typical $Δτ$ between 30 and 80 Myr. However, gas-rich interactions can disrupt this age offset, resulting in similar age distributions on each side of the spiral arms. In three snapshots, we observe similar mean ages on both sides of spiral arms but differing age distribution broadness, coinciding with satellite interactions crossing the host galaxy's disc plane. Our simulation data suggest that the typical azimuthal age variation recovers within ~600 Myr after galaxy interactions. This work highlights the transient role of environmental interactions in shaping spiral arm age patterns.

### [5] [The impact of selection criteria on the properties of green valley galaxies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20379)
*Beatrice Nyiransengiyumva,Mirjana Povic,Pheneas Nkundabakura,Tom Mutabazi,Antoine Mahoro*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 该研究首次系统评估了绿谷星系选择标准的影响，发现不同标准会选择不同类型的星系，建议避免仅基于颜色的选择方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 绿谷星系作为星系从晚型向早型演化的过渡区域，其选择对研究星系形态转变至关重要。然而，目前最常用的绿谷选择标准存在选择效应，可能影响对星系演化的理解。

Method: 使用SDSS光学数据和GALEX紫外数据（红移z<0.1），基于颜色、比恒星形成率、恒星形成率vs恒星质量等8种最常用标准选择绿谷星系，并分析其性质差异。

Result: 不同标准选择的星系类型不同：紫外-光学颜色标准倾向于选择质量更大、恒星形成率更低、复合星系和椭圆星系比例更高的星系；颜色标准对星系性质变化最敏感。

Conclusion: 建议尽可能避免仅基于颜色的绿谷选择方法，改用恒星形成率vs恒星质量或比恒星形成率等其他方法，或多种方法的组合。

Abstract: Context: The bi-modality in the distribution of galaxies usually obtained from colour-colour or colour-stellar mass diagrams has been studied to show the difference between the galaxies in the blue cloud and in the red sequence and to define the green valley region. As a transition region, the green valley galaxies can give clues about morphological transformation of galaxies from late- to early-types, and therefore the selection of green valley is of fundamental importance. Aims: In this work, for the first time, we evaluate the selection effects of the most used green valley selection criteria. The aim is to understand how these criteria affect the identification of green valley galaxies, their properties, and their impact on galaxy evolution studies. Methods: Using the SDSS optical and GALEX ultraviolet data at redshift z < 0.1, we selected the eight most commonly used criteria based on colours, specific star formation rate, and star formation rate vs. stellar mass. We then studied the properties of the green valley galaxies (their stellar mass, star formation rate, specific star formation rate, intrinsic brightness, morphological and
  pectroscopic types) for each selection criterion. Results: We found that when using different criteria, we select different types of galaxies. UV-optical colour-based criteria tend to select more massive galaxies, with lower star formation rates, with higher fractions of composite and elliptical galaxies, than when using pure optical colours. Our results also show that the colour-based criteria are the most sensitive to galaxy properties, rapidly changing the selection of green valley galaxies. Conclusions: Whenever possible, we suggest avoiding the green valley colour-based selection and using other methods or a combination of several, such as the star formation rate vs. stellar mass or specific star formation rate.
