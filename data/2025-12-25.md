### [1] [BASS.L. Near-infrared Data Release 3: A Spectral Atlas and Characterization of AGN](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20702)
*Jarred Gillette,Michael J. Koss,Darshan Kakkad,Federica Ricci,Isabella Lamperti,Kyuseok Oh,Alejandra Rojas,Yaherlyn Diaz,Turgay Caglar,Kohei Ichikawa,Ignacio del Moral-Castro,Claudio Ricci,Ezequiel Treister,Franz E. Bauer,Richard Mushotzky,Mislav Baloković,Jakob S. den Brok,Benny Trakhtenbrot,C. Megan Urry,Fiona Harrison,Daniel Stern*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: BASS DR3 NIR光谱研究显示，近红外发射线（特别是SiVI）比光学[OIII]能更可靠地示踪AGN光度，并提出了改进的NIR诊断图来区分AGN、恒星形成和复合源。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 本研究旨在利用BASS DR3的大样本硬X射线选AGN近红外光谱数据，系统分析AGN的近红外发射线特性、诊断方法以及核周气体动力学，以更好地示踪AGN活动、探测被遮蔽的宽线区，并理解AGN与核周环境的相互作用。

Method: 使用VLT X-shooter多波长光谱仪（0.3-2.5μm）对453个硬X射线选AGN（14-195 keV）进行观测，采用≥2σ检测阈值，分析近红外发射线特性、AGN诊断和核周气体动力学。

Result: 1) NIR日冕线（特别是SiVI λ1.964）比光学[OIII] λ5007与硬X射线光度相关性更紧密（σ=0.25 vs 0.55 dex）；2) 宽帕邢线在12%的Seyfert 2和57%的Seyfert 1.9中被探测到；3) 提出了改进的NIR诊断图（FeII λ1.257μm/Paβ和H2 λ2.122μm/Brγ）；4) 热分子气体质量与X射线光度存在中度相关，但与爱丁顿比关系较弱；5) 热-冷气体质量比跨越四个数量级，平均约3×10⁻⁷。

Conclusion: 近红外光谱是探测AGN活动、被遮蔽宽线区以及AGN与核周环境复杂相互作用的有效工具，NIR发射线特别是日冕线能更可靠地示踪AGN光度，而改进的诊断图有助于在污染或只有上限数据时准确区分不同源类型。

Abstract: We present an analysis of near-infrared (NIR) emission-line properties, AGN diagnostics, and circumnuclear gas dynamics for 453 hard X-ray selected (14$-$195 keV) AGN from the BAT AGN Spectroscopic Survey (BASS) NIR Data Release 3 (DR3; $\langle z \rangle=0.036$, $z<1.0$). This dataset is the largest compilation of rest-frame NIR spectroscopic observations of hard-X-ray-selected AGN and includes the full DR2 sample. Observations were obtained with VLT X-shooter, a multiwavelength (0.3$-$2.5$μ$m) spectrograph ($R$ = 4,000$-$18,000), using a $\geq 2 σ$ detection threshold, enabling broad analysis of emission features. We find that NIR coronal lines, particularly SiVI $\lambda1.964$, are more reliable tracers of AGN luminosity than optical [OIII], showing a tighter correlation with hard X-ray luminosity ($σ=0.25$dex) than [OIII] $\lambda5007$ ($σ=0.55$dex). Broad Paschen lines (Pa$α$ and Pa$β$) are detected in 12% of Seyfert 2 and 57% of Seyfert 1.9, consistent with previous hidden BLR studies. We introduce a refined NIR diagnostic diagram (FeII $\lambda1.257$$μ$m/Pa$β$ and H$_2$ $λ2.122$$μ$m/Br$γ$) that effectively distinguishes AGN, star-forming, and composite sources even when contamination limits individual diagnostics or only upper limits are available. Additionally, we find a moderate correlation ($p \approx 7.4 \times 10^{-3}$) between hot molecular gas mass (traced by H$_2$ 2.121$μ$m) and X-ray luminosity, while its relation with Eddington ratio is weaker. The hot-to-cold gas mass ratio spans four orders of magnitude, averaging $\sim 3 \times 10^{-7}$, indicating diverse molecular gas excitation processes likely driven by star formation and AGN feedback. Our results underscore the value of NIR spectroscopy in probing AGN activity, obscured BLRs, and the complex interactions between AGN and their circumnuclear environments.

### [2] [Bar Formation in Disc Galaxies: Internal Kinematics and Environmental Influence in MaNGA Galaxies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21303)
*Erik Aquino-Ortíz,Bernardo Cervantes-Sodi,Karol Chim-Ramirez*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 研究利用SDSS-IV MaNGA巡天数据，分析棒旋星系物理特性，发现棒旋星系与中心恒星质量集中度更高、角动量更低相关，且棒形成主要受内部引力不稳定性驱动，外部潮汐扰动起次要作用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究棒旋星系物理特性与环境关系，验证理论模型，理解棒形成机制（内部过程vs外部扰动）。

Method: 使用SDSS-IV MaNGA巡天数据，结合内部运动学特性和环境诊断，分析棒旋星系与无棒星系在恒星质量分布、角动量等方面的差异。

Result: 棒旋星系具有更中心集中的恒星质量分布（在1-2有效半径内），角动量λ_Re更低；棒比例与最近邻潮汐相互作用呈双峰依赖关系；内部引力不稳定性是棒形成的主要驱动力。

Conclusion: 棒形成主要受内部引力不稳定性驱动，外部潮汐扰动起次要触发或增强作用，为理论模型提供了观测验证。

Abstract: We explore how the physical properties of disc galaxies relate to the presence of bars using data from the SDSS-IV MaNGA survey. By combining internal kinematical properties and environmental diagnostics, we find that barred galaxies are more frequently associated with centrally concentrated stellar mass distributions (within 1 and 2 effective radii) and exhibit lower values of the stellar angular momentum $λ_{Re}$. At fixed total stellar mass, barred galaxies exhibit: (i) higher stellar mass, and (ii) lower angular momentum, both in their inner regions than their unbarred counterparts. We find a bimodal dependence of the bar fraction on tidal interactions produced by the nearest neighbour. Specifically, the bar fraction peaks in the most isolated galaxies, where bars form unequivocally through internal secular processes, decreases at intermediate interaction strengths, and rises again in the strong interaction regime, likely reflecting the role of dense environments in sustaining or triggering bars. Our results suggest that internal gravitational instabilities are the primary driver of bar formation. External tidal perturbations play a secondary role, capable of triggering or enhancing bar formation in galaxies that are already internally predisposed. Our findings provide robust observational validation of theoretical bar formation and evolution models in galaxies.
