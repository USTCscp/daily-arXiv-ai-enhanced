### [1] [A Velocity Coupled Radial Acceleration Ansatz for Disk-Galaxy Rotation Curves: Fits to SPARC, Bayesian Inference, and Parameter Identifiability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21376)
*Nalin Dhiman*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 研究提出了一种最小化的现象学替代模型（VCA），通过在标准暗晕参数化基础上增加与局部切向速度成正比的径向加速度，用于拟合星系旋转曲线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 星系旋转曲线是研究重子物质与动力学关系的重要观测探针，需要探索标准暗晕参数化之外的现象学替代模型。

Method: 提出速度耦合加速度（VCA）模型：a_c(r)=γ(r)v(r)，其中耦合系数γ(r)=v_∞/(r+r_0)。结合圆周运动条件得到v(r)的二次方程，使用SPARC样本的171个旋转曲线进行拟合，并与NFW和Burkert暗晕模型比较。

Result: 在5 km/s系统误差下，VCA模型通常与NFW暗晕竞争，与Burkert暗晕表现相当（但不总是更好）。参数后验显示v_∞和r_0存在强简并性，仅47/171星系有良好识别的后验分布。径向保留交叉验证显示预测RMSE与NFW和Burkert相当。

Conclusion: VCA模型作为最小化现象学替代方案，在拟合星系旋转曲线方面表现与标准暗晕模型相当，但参数识别存在挑战，需要进一步研究。

Abstract: Observed rotation curves of disk galaxies remain a sharp empirical probe of the relationship between baryons and dynamics. We study a minimal, explicitly \emph{phenomenological} alternative to standard halo parameterizations: an additional inward \emph{radial} acceleration proportional to the local \emph{tangential} speed, $a_{\vca}(r)=γ(r)\,v(r)$, with a saturating coupling $γ(r)=\vinf/(r+\rzero)$. Combining this ansatz with the circular-motion condition yields a quadratic equation for $v(r)$ with a closed-form physical branch.We fit this ``velocity-coupled acceleration'' (\vca) model to $N_\mathrm{gal}=171$ rotation curves from the SPARC sample using the published baryonic decompositions (gas, disk, bulge), and we compare to two commonly used two-parameter halo models (NFW and Burkert) using an identical optimization pipeline and error model.For a fiducial systematic error floor $σ_0=5~\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, the \vca model is typically competitive with an NFW halo and performs comparably (though not uniformly better) than a Burkert halo in information-criterion comparisons.We further perform MCMC inference for \vca parameters, quantify posterior predictive coverage, and show that parameter posteriors exhibit a strong $\vinf$--$\rzero$ degeneracy for many galaxies; only $47/171$ galaxies yield well-identified posteriors under simple width-based criteria. We also perform a simple radial holdout cross-validation (outer 30\% of radii) and find predictive RMSE comparable to NFW and Burkert under this protocol.Finally..

### [2] [Can A Kinematically Hot and Thick Disk Form A Bar? : Role of Highly Spinning Dark Matter Halos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21632)
*Sandeep Kumar Kataria*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: JWST观测发现高红移宇宙中存在大量棒旋结构，这与当前对银河系类似星系盘稳定性的理解相矛盾。研究表明，在旋转暗晕环境中，经典稳定的热厚盘也能形成棒结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: JWST观测显示高红移宇宙中热厚盘存在大量棒旋结构，这与传统理论认为热厚盘对棒形成稳定的观点相矛盾，需要重新理解高红移星系的棒形成机制。

Method: 使用受控N体模拟研究热厚盘在不同暗晕环境中的稳定性，比较非旋转暗晕和旋转暗晕对盘稳定性的影响，分析角动量传输效率。

Result: 旋转暗晕模型比非旋转暗晕在角动量传输上效率高8倍，导致经典稳定的热厚盘在旋转暗晕环境中形成棒结构。现有棒形成判据（Ostriker-Peebles、ELN）无法预测旋转暗晕中的棒形成，Jang-Kim判据仅能预测非旋转暗晕情况。

Conclusion: 旋转暗晕通过高效角动量传输促进了热厚盘的棒形成，这为解释高红移宇宙中观测到的棒旋结构提供了重要机制，表明需要重新评估现有棒稳定性判据的适用性。

Abstract: Recent JWST observations claim the existence of a significant fraction of bars in the kinematically hotter and thicker disk at high redshift Universe. These observations challenge the current understanding of disk stability in galaxies similar to the Milky Way. The analytical work and N-body simulations suggest that the kinematically hot (dispersion-dominated) and thick disk are stable against bar formation. In this work, we perform the controlled N-body simulations of a kinematically hot and thick disk, which is residing in a non-rotating and spinning dark matter halo. We report that the disk, which is classically stable against bar instability in the live and non-rotating halo, leads to bar formation in a spinning halo environment. The spinning halo model is 8 times more efficient in transporting angular momentum from the disk to the halo compared to the non-spinning halo. We claim that Ostriker-Peebles and ELN bar formation criteria do not predict bar formation for both the non-roating and spinning halo. The recent criteria from Jang-Kim successfully predict the bar stability for the non-rotating halo model, but not for the spinning halo model. These results provide an important insight into the bar formation processes for thick and hot disks at high redshift.

### [3] [A systematic study on the aromatic and aliphatic hydrocarbon emission features of nearby galaxies using AKARI near-IR spectra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21674)
*Tsubasa Kondo,Hidehiro Kaneda,Shinki Oyabu,Takuma Kokusho,Toyoaki Suzuki,Risako Katayama,Eiko Kozaki,Itsuka Yachi,Keita Yoshida,Shohei Ono*

TL;DR: 研究使用AKARI/IRC观测的3.3μm和3.4-3.6μm近红外光谱特征，系统分析了星际烃类尘埃（特别是芳香烃和脂肪烃发射特征的光度比变化原因），覆盖了不同红外光度星系和银河系HII区域样本。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 星际烃类尘埃（如多环芳烃PAHs）在银河系环境中会受到紫外辐射场和机械冲击等多种因素的处理，但导致芳香烃和脂肪烃发射特征光度比变化的具体原因尚不清楚，需要系统研究。

Method: 使用AKARI/IRC观测的3.3μm和3.4-3.6μm近红外光谱特征，分析了243个近红外光谱（240个恒星形成(U)LIRGs）、119个光谱（105个恒星形成IRGs）、94个光谱（65个sub-IRGs）以及232个光谱（36个银河系HII区域作为参考样本）。通过近红外光谱模型拟合估计芳香烃和脂肪烃特征以及Brα氢复合线的光度。

Result: 样本星系中脂肪烃与芳香烃的光度比（L_ali/L_aro）变化显著大于银河系HII区域，且该比值随总红外光度（L_IR）和Brα光度（L_Brα）系统性地降低。4μm连续谱颜色较蓝的(sub-)IRGs倾向于具有更高的L_ali/L_aro，而一些4μm连续谱颜色较红的ULIRGs显示出极低的L_ali/L_aro。

Abstract: Interstellar hydrocarbon dust containing aromatic and aliphatic hydrocarbons, like polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), is believed to be processed by various factors including UV radiation fields and mechanical shocks in the galactic environments. We performed near-IR spectral model fitting to estimate the luminosities of the aromatic and aliphatic hydrocarbon features and the HI recombination line Br$\rmα$. The result indicates that the luminosity ratios of the aliphatic to the aromatic hydrocarbons ($L_\rm{ali}/L_\rm{aro}$) in the sample galaxies show considerably large variations, compared to those in the Galactic HII regions, $L_\rm{ali}/L_\rm{aro}$ systematically decreasing with $L_\rm{IR}$ and $L_\rm{Brα}$. We find that (sub-)IRGs with continuum colors bluer at 4 um tend to have higher $L_\rm{ali}/L_\rm{aro}$, which is likely to reflect the intrinsic nature of PAHs outside the HII region where the PAHs remain non-processed by strong UV radiation fields. We also find that some ULIRGs with continuum colors redder at 4 um show extremely low $L_\rm{ali}/L_\rm{aro}$, which is likely to be caused by blending aliphatic emission and absorption features due to the presence of an obscured galactic nucleus in merger systems.

### [4] [The rotation curve of the Milky Way measured by Classical Cepheids from Gaia DR3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21780)
*Qikang Feng,Yang Huang,Huawei Zhang,Jifeng Liu*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 利用903颗经典造父变星构建银河系旋转曲线，发现10-11千秒差距处有凹陷、13-14千秒差距处有凸起特征，测得太阳位置圆周速度为236.8±0.8 km/s，估算局部暗物质密度和18千秒差距内暗物质晕质量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 精确测量银河系旋转曲线对于理解银河系质量分布、暗物质分布以及星系动力学至关重要。先前研究虽然已有旋转曲线测量，但不同示踪天体得到的结果存在差异，特别是年轻示踪天体（如造父变星）显示的旋转曲线存在特殊特征，需要更精确的数据来验证这些特征并约束银河系质量模型。

Method: 使用Gaia DR3数据中903颗经典造父变星，这些天体具有精确的自行运动和高质量径向速度。通过已知的周期-韦森海特关系准确测量距离，利用三维速度分量计算旋转曲线。为更好约束银河系质量，将本研究结果与先前测量结合构建平均旋转曲线，并在受特征影响较小的径向范围内进行参数化质量建模。

Result: 旋转曲线总体上随距银心距离增加而轻微下降，但在R~10-11千秒差距处出现凹陷，在R~13-14千秒差距处出现凸起。太阳位置圆周速度为236.8±0.8 km/s，与先前测量一致。局部暗物质密度为0.33-0.40 GeV/cm³，18千秒差距内暗物质晕质量为1.19-1.45×10¹¹ M⊙。

Conclusion: 本研究利用高质量造父变星数据构建了精确的银河系旋转曲线，确认了年轻示踪天体旋转曲线中存在的凹陷-凸起特征。通过结合先前测量构建平均旋转曲线，得到了精确的太阳位置圆周速度和银河系质量参数，为理解银河系质量分布和暗物质特性提供了重要约束。

Abstract: We determine the rotation curve (RC) of the Milky Way in the range 6 < R < 18 kpc using a sample of 903 carefully selected classical Cepheids with precise proper motions and high-quality radial velocities from \emph{Gaia} DR3. Their distances can be accurately measured from the well-known Period-Wesenheit relations. The RC is computed from the three-dimensional velocity components of these Cepheids. Generally, the RC shows a slight decline with distance from the Galactic center. On top of this general trend, the newly constructed RC shows a dip around R ~ 10-11 kpc, followed by a bump around R ~ 13-14 kpc. This feature has also been reported in other RC measurements, mostly in RCs traced by young tracers like Cepheids. To better constrain the Milky Way mass, an averaged RC is then constructed by combining measurements from this work and previous efforts. Due to the ambiguous nature of the dip-and-bump feature, this averaged RC is constructed only within the radial range where the RC appears to be less influenced by this feature. By using this averaged RC, we determine the circular velocity at solar position and also build a parameterized mass model of our Milky Way. The result for the circular velocity at the solar position is $V_c(R_0) = 236.8 \pm 0.8\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}}$, which is in good agreement with previous measurements. The local dark matter density and the enclosed dark matter halo mass within 18 kpc are estimated from the averaged RC under different baryonic models, yielding a series of consistent results: a local density of $0.33-0.40\ \mathrm{GeV\,cm^{-3}}$ and an enclosed mass of $1.19-1.45 \times 10^{11}\ M_\odot$.

### [5] [A large, long-lived, slowly-expanding superbubble across the Perseus Arm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21927)

TL;DR: 结合Gaia和LAMOST数据研究银河系最大超级气泡，发现O-B2星群以25.8 km/s横向速度和6.2 km/s膨胀速度运动，表明重复超新星能量足以维持气泡生长，形成类似JWST观测的准稳态超级气泡。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究恒星反馈在星系演化中的关键作用，特别是通过JWST观测到的广泛气泡现象，探索银河系最大超级气泡的形成和维持机制。

Method: 结合Gaia和LAMOST数据获取具有完整三维速度信息的年轻O-B2星样本，聚焦银河系最大超级气泡，分析其外围星群的运动特征和时间尺度。

Result: 发现O-B2星群具有25.8 km/s横向速度和6.2 km/s膨胀速度，计算得到穿越时间约20 Myr、膨胀时间约80 Myr、生存时间约250 Myr、超新星间隔约0.1 Myr，满足t_SN < t_shear < t_survival关系。

Abstract: Stellar feedback is a crucial mechanism in galactic evolution, as demonstrated by the widespread bubbles observed with JWST. In this study, we combine data from Gaia and LAMOST to obtain a sample of young O-B2 stars with full three-dimensional velocity information. Focusing on the largest known superbubble in the Milky Way, we identify groups of O-B2 stars at its periphery, exhibiting a transverse velocity of 25.8 km/s and an expansion velocity of 6.2 km/s. Using these velocities, we calculate a crossing time t_cross ~ 20 Myr and an expansion timescale t_expansion ~ 80 Myr. We estimate a survival timescale t_survival ~ 250 Myr and a supernova interval t_SN ~ 0.1 Myr. Together with the Galactic shear timescale t_shear ~ 30 Myr, these values satisfy t_SN < t_shear < t_survival. The energy and momentum from supernovae are sufficient to sustain the bubble's growth against ambient pressure. This indicates that repeated supernovae replenish energy faster than shear and turbulent distort the cavity. Our analysis classifies the Giant Oval Cavity as a large, quasi-stationary superbubble, similar to the Phantom Bubble observed by JWST, stabilised by the interplay between stellar feedback and Galactic disk dynamics.
