Motivation: 研究星系相互作用如何影响棒结构演化，通过比较孤立星系和星系对中的棒比例，了解潮汐相互作用对星系结构的影响机制。

Method: 使用SDSS数据，分析星系对中的棒比例，比较不同投影距离（特别是25kpc内）的星系对与孤立星系，考察合并特征、棒强度、星系质量、并合类型等因素的影响。

Result: 紧密星系对（<25kpc）的棒比例显著降低，这种降低主要由显示明显合并或扰动特征的系统驱动；弱棒比例下降明显，强棒比例基本不变；抑制效应在主要合并中最为显著，且主要出现在大质量宿主星系中；次要合并仅在大质量、小核球星系中显示微弱抑制；棒抑制与星系对相对方向无关。

Conclusion: 观测证据表明，主要合并中的潮汐扰动在调控棒演化中起关键作用，星系相互作用特别是主要合并事件会显著抑制棒结构的形成和维持。

Abstract: We investigate the bar fraction in galaxy pairs from the SDSS to assess how galaxy interactions affect bar structures. Compared to isolated galaxies, close pairs exhibit a significantly reduced bar fraction at projected separations within 25 kpc. This reduction is driven almost entirely by systems showing clear merger or disturbance signatures, indicating that tidal interactions suppress bars. The decline is dominated by a decrease in weak bars, while the fraction of strong bars remains largely unchanged. Bar suppression is primarily associated with major mergers and is strongest in massive host galaxies. A weaker but statistically significant suppression is detected in minor mergers only for massive galaxies with small bulges. In contrast, no significant dependence of bar suppression on the relative orientation between pair members is found. These findings provide observational evidence that tidal perturbations in major mergers play a key role in regulating bar evolution.
