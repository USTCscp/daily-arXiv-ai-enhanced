Method: 通过模拟BDS模型下的透镜化双黑洞合并，并与观测数据对比，检验模型是否满足：1)观测到的双黑洞合并数量；2)红移总质量和视光度距离分布；3)强透镜事件未探测到的情况；4)随机引力波背景未观测到的现象。

Method: 使用SDSS DR7数据，通过多分量结构分解获得可靠光谱指数，利用4000Å断裂强度(D_n(4000))和巴尔默吸收指数(Hδ_A)追踪恒星年龄和近期恒星形成，结合核球、棒和盘的颜色分析

Result: 棒旋星系在D_n(4000)上呈现明显双峰分布，低D_n(4000)伪核球显示年轻、活跃的恒星形成，高D_n(4000)系统则为年老、熄灭的伪核球；棒状S0星系显示老核球主导分布；年老伪核球中AGN发生率较高

Conclusion: 棒结构通过向内输送气体构建伪核球，随后通过耗尽或稳定流入来抑制中心恒星形成，棒驱动的长期演化既调控中心组件的增长也调控其熄灭，连接了蓝色棒旋星系与红色S0星系

Abstract: We investigate the stellar population properties of pseudo-bulges in barred galaxies drawn from the Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS DR7) to assess how bars regulate central star formation and secular evolution. Our sample comprises barred spiral and barred lenticular (S0) galaxies with reliable spectroscopic indices obtained from multicomponent structural decompositions. Stellar ages and recent star formation are traced using the 4000 Å break strength ($D_{n}(4000)$) and the Balmer absorption index ($Hδ_{A}$), complemented by bulge, bar, and disc colours. Barred spirals show a clear bimodality in $D_{n}(4000)$, with peaks at $D_{n}(4000)\sim1.3$ and $\sim1.8$. Low-$D_{n}(4000)$ pseudo-bulges exhibit strong $Hδ_{A}$ absorption, blue colours, and high specific star-formation rates, indicating young, actively growing centres. High-$D_{n}(4000)$ systems instead show weak $Hδ_{A}$, red colours, and low sSFR, consistent with older, quenched pseudo-bulges. Barred S0s display an old-bulge-dominated distribution, suggesting that gas-poor barred spirals transition into S0s following disc-wide quenching. We also find elevated AGN incidence among old pseudo-bulges. These trends support a scenario in which bars funnel gas inward to build pseudo-bulges and later suppress central star formation by depleting or stabilising the inflow. IFU observations show that bars assemble cold nuclear discs that age and quench over time, while high-redshift imaging confirms that bars are already present at $z\sim4$, implying that this evolutionary cycle operates across cosmic time. The strong correspondence between stellar age, colour, and structure indicates that bar-driven secular evolution governs both the growth and quenching of central components, linking blue barred spirals to red S0 galaxies.
