*V. V. Bobylev,A. T. Bajkova*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 本文研究了本地系统中年龄小于3000万年的疏散星团（OSCs）的空间分布，首次发现大量OSCs属于新发现的Vela Ridge气体尘埃超云。这些OSC样本最引人注目的特征是垂直坐标存在周期性扰动，最大振幅47 pc，波长1.1 kpc，形成了一条类似Radcliffe Wave但振幅更低、波长更短、平均年龄大200万年的年轻OSC链。

Motivation: 探索年轻疏散星团在本地系统中的空间分布特征，特别是发现并表征其与宇宙云结构的关联以及垂直方向上的周期性扰动现象。

Method: 对年龄小于3000万年的疏散星团进行空间分布统计分析，将其与Vela Ridge气体尘埃超云进行空间位置关联，并对星团垂直坐标进行周期性扰动分析。

Result: 首次发现大量年轻OSCs属于Vela Ridge超云；OSC垂直坐标存在周期性扰动，最大振幅47 pc，波长1.1 kpc，形成了类似Radcliffe Wave但振幅更低、波长更短的星团链。

Abstract: The spatial distribution of open star clusters (OSCs) younger than 30 million years old in the Local System was studied. It was shown for the first time that a significant number of OSCs belong to the recently discovered Vela Ridge gas and dust supercloud. The most intriguing property of this sample of OSCs is the presence of periodic perturbations in their vertical coordinates with a maximum amplitude of 47 pc and a wavelength of 1.1 kpc. Thus, the discovered chain of young OSCs is analogous to the Radcliffe Wave, but with a lower amplitude of vertical perturbations, a shorter wavelength, and is, on average, 2 million years older.
