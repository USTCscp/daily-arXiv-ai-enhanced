Abstract: The methylidyne cation forms in warm or shocked interstellar gas through the endothermic reaction C$^+$ + H$_2$ -> CH$^+$ + H. JWST/MIRI observations of HH 168 reveal the pure rotational lines R(17)-R(31) of CH$^+$ at 13.7-21.3 micron, from upper levels J = 18-32 (0.57-1.65 eV). A Boltzmann distribution at $φT$ ~ 6200 K, where $φ$ is the fraction of the available energy appearing in rotation, fits slightly better, implying reactant energies equivalent to several thousand kelvin. These lines probe the dynamics of a prototypical interstellar ion-molecule reaction.
