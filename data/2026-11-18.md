Motivation: ω Cen 是银河系中最重的球状星团，可能是被吸积矮星系的残余核，其复杂星族结构与中心快速运动恒星提供的中等质量黑洞动力学证据都需要高精度天体测量来约束恒星轨道。已有 HST 观测在暗星端精度与完备性受限，因此需要扩展到近红外波段以获得更高的灵敏度与角分辨率。

Method: 对三个 JWST 项目的 NIRCam 与 NIRISS 成像进行自定义归算，覆盖 ω Cen 核心及周边直至两个半光半径（10 角分）的区域，构建测光与天体测量星表；通过与既有 oMEGACat（HST）星表交叉比对评估相对完备性，并利用单图像天体测量检验给出精度随星等的变化，同时构建颜色-星等图分析星族分裂与轻元素变化。

Result: 星表共包含约 677 000 个源；在重叠区域内 HST 源回收率超过 90%，同时在 HST 探测极限以下新发现超过 86 000 个源。单图像天体测量精度在 mF115W=18 时达 0.5% 个 NIRCam 像素（0.15 mas），在 mF115W=23 时仍为 1 mas。颜色-星等图清晰显示原初氦与富氦星族的分裂，并对暗星的 CNO 变化高度敏感。

Conclusion: oMEGACat-JWST 是目前 ω Cen 核心区最完备的测光星表，与 HST 数据结合可实现多波段星族分析；其高天体测量精度结合后续监测观测，将提升对中心快速运动恒星动力学的认识，并有助于检验中等质量黑洞的存在。

Abstract: Omega Centauri (NGC 5139, $ω$ Cen) is the most massive globular cluster in the Milky Way and the likely stripped nucleus of an accreted dwarf galaxy. Extensive HST observations have uncovered many of its peculiar features, including complex subpopulations and kinematic evidence for an intermediate-mass black hole via central fast-moving stars. High-precision astrometric follow-up is essential to constrain the orbits of these stars. We present oMEGACat-JWST, a deep photometric and astrometric catalog of $ω$ Cen's core and surroundings that extends our previous oMEGACat efforts to near-infrared wavelengths. The superior sensitivity and angular resolution of the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) provide significant astrometric and photometric improvements, particularly for faint stars. Our catalog is based on a custom reduction of NIRCam and NIRISS imaging across three JWST programs extending out to two half-light radii (10'). In total, our catalog contains approximately 677 000 sources. In the region overlapping with oMEGACat, we achieve high relative completeness (> 90% of HST sources recovered) while detecting over 86 000 new sources below HST's detection limit. Astrometric tests demonstrate a single-image precision as good as 0.5% of a NIRCam pixel (0.15 mas) at mF115W=18; at mF115W=23 the precision is still 1 mas. Color-magnitude diagrams based on our data show the well-known split between the primordial Helium and the Helium enriched populations and are particularly sensitive to light-element (CNO) variations of faint stars. oMEGACat-JWST provides the most complete photometric catalog of $ω$ Cen's core to date. Combined with HST data, it enables multi-wavelength subpopulation analyses. Together with future monitoring observations, its high astrometric precision will improve our understanding of the kinematics of the central fast-moving stars in $ω$ Cen.
