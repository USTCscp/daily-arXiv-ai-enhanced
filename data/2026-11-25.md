### [1] [Spectroscopic identification of z>7 quasars with Roman Space Telescope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31352)
*Mainak Singha,Wei Leong Tee,Sangeeta Malhotra,Isak Wold,James Rhoads,Xiaohui Fan*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 该文通过模拟Roman宽场仪器无缝光谱对z=7.5-10类星体Lyman-alpha断裂的探测能力，给出断裂完备度随J波段星等的变化（J~23.7-24.1保持>90%，~24.3-24.7降至50%），并检验了与褐矮星、红星系的光谱分类区分度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Roman宽场仪器的棱栅覆盖1.0-1.93微米的大面积区域，使直接搜寻红移z>7类星体的Lyman-alpha断裂成为可能，但需要评估这一无缝光谱巡天在高红移类星体证认上的实际探测能力与污染风险。

Method: 用ESpRESSO模拟HLWAS类曝光下z=7.5-10的类星体光谱，包含PSF卷积、无缝色散、源与背景泊松噪声、读出噪声及Horne最优提取；采用三种Lyman-alpha等值宽（EW）方案以涵盖线强不确定性；提取谱与M/L/T矮星及被动红星系谱用光谱角映射（SAM）比较分类。

Result: Lyman-alpha断裂完备度在J_AB≈23.7-24.1处仍高于90%，在J_AB≈24.3-24.7处降至50%；J_AB≳25后结果强烈依赖所采用的EW分布。固定红移SAM测试中类星体找回率TPR≥0.982，且无含噪褐矮星或红星系测试谱被误判为类星体；但样本源自同一有限母库，因此该结果反映库内抗噪稳定性而非独立标定的误报率。暗端最大的天体物理敏感性来自Lyman-alpha分布的高EW尾部。

Conclusion: Roman棱栅在J_AB约24等量级上可高效、较纯净地证认z>7类星体，暗端性能受Lyman-alpha高EW尾支配；未来工作需以独立样本标定真实误报率。

Abstract: The Roman Wide Field Instrument grism will cover a large area over 1.0-1.93 micrometers, making it possible to search directly for Lyman-alpha breaks in quasars beyond redshift z = 7. We simulate quasars at z = 7.5-10 under HLWAS-like exposures with ESpRESSO, including PSF convolution, slitless dispersion, source and background Poisson noise, read noise, and Horne optimal extraction. Three Lyman-alpha equivalent-width (EW) prescriptions are used to bracket the uncertainty in line strength, and the extracted spectra are compared with M/L/T dwarfs and passive red galaxies using the spectral angle mapper. The Lyman-alpha-break completeness remains above 90% to J_AB approximately 23.7-24.1 and reaches 50% at J_AB approximately 24.3-24.7. Beyond J_AB approximately 25, the result depends strongly on the adopted EW distribution. In the fixed-redshift SAM test, the QSO recovery rate is TPR >= 0.982. None of the noisy brown-dwarf or red-galaxy test spectra is classified as a QSO. Because those test spectra are generated from the same finite parent libraries as the reference classes, this result measures the stability of the classification against noise within those libraries rather than an independently calibrated survey false-positive rate. At the faint end, the strongest astrophysical sensitivity in the present calculation is to the high-EW tail of the Lyman-alpha distribution.

### [2] [JWST/NIRSpec observations of AMORE6 suggest active Pop~III star formation in a self-polluted galaxy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31355)

### [3] [AGN feedback in the multiphase inner intracluster medium of a cosmological super-resolved galaxy cluster](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31367)
*Marine Prunier,Rahul Ramesh,Dylan Nelson,Annalisa Pillepich,Julie Hlavacek-Larrondo,Katrin Lehle,Joey Braspenning*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 利用TNG-Cluster初始条件和IllustrisTNG模型，对低质量冷核星系团进行宇宙学放大磁流体模拟，在约40–90 pc分辨率下研究冷气体云/纤维与AGN反馈的相互作用，发现AGN反馈打碎并重新分布冷气体、提高其覆盖度而不摧毁冷气体储库，且单次爆发在≲50 Myr时间尺度上短暂提升冷暖气体速度与速度弥散。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 星系团核心的星系团内介质是多相环境，由AGN反馈调节；冷核星系团中普遍观测到形态和运动学复杂的延展冷气体星云，但其起源及其与AGN反馈的相互作用仍不清楚。以往模拟难以在保留完整宇宙学语境的同时分辨冷气相的小尺度结构。

Method: 采用宇宙学磁流体动力学放大（zoom-in）模拟，对象为低质量冷核星系团（M_200c = 2.3×10^14 M_⊙）；初始条件取自TNG-Cluster，星系形成模型为IllustrisTNG；通过超拉格朗日精化，在中心370 ckpc范围内冷气相（10^4 K < T < 10^5.5 K）达到2.3×10^4 M_⊙的重子质量分辨率和约40–90 pc的空间分辨率；在z ≤ 0.35分析冷云与纤维的性质与演化，并对比AGN动能反馈开启与关闭两种模拟。

Result: 星系团核心存在大量致密低熵纤维结构，包括内流与外流结构及偶发的旋转支撑盘。对比AGN反馈开/关：AGN活动打碎并重新分布冷气体、增加其覆盖度，但不摧毁冷气体储库；单次爆发在≲50 Myr的短时间尺度上引起冷、热气体速度与速度弥散的瞬态增加；识别出由AGN驱动外流抬升的低熵气体。

Abstract: The intracluster medium (ICM) of galaxy cluster cores is a multiphase environment regulated by active galactic nuclei (AGN) feedback. Extended cool gas nebulae with complex morphologies and kinematics are commonly observed in cool-core clusters, but their origin and interaction with AGN feedback remain poorly understood. We present a cosmological magnetohydrodynamical zoom-in simulation of a low-mass cool-core galaxy cluster ($M_{\mathrm{200c}}=2.3\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{M}_\odot$), using initial conditions from TNG-Cluster and the IllustrisTNG galaxy formation model. Through super-Lagrangian refinement, we achieve a baryonic mass resolution of $2.3\times10^{4}\,\mathrm{M}_\odot$ and a spatial resolution of $\sim40\text{-}90$ pc in the cool ($10^{4}\,\mathrm{K}<T<10^{5.5}\,\mathrm{K}$) gas phase within the central $370$ ckpc, while retaining the full cosmological context. We investigate the properties and evolution of cool clouds and filaments and their interaction with AGN feedback at $z\leq0.35$. The cluster core hosts a rich population of dense, low-entropy filaments, including inflowing and outflowing structures and occasional rotationally supported disks. Comparing simulations with AGN kinetic feedback turned off and on, we find that AGN activity fragments and redistributes the cool gas and increases its covering fraction without destroying the cool gas reservoir. Individual outbursts drive transient increases in the velocities and velocity dispersions of both cool and hot gas, on short, $\lesssim 50$ Myr, timescales. We also identify low-entropy gas uplifted by AGN-driven outflows. While feedback globally heats the ICM and offsets cooling, it also creates conditions that may favor local thermal instabilities -- through shock compressions, mixing, turbulence, and uplift.
